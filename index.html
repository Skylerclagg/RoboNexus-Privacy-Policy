RoboNexus Privacy Policy

Effective October 23, 2026

RoboNexus is a free app by Skyler Clagg for following REC Foundation and VEX robotics competitions. This policy explains what the app collects, why, and how to delete it. RoboNexus does not sell your data, does not show ads, and does not track you across other apps or websites.

The short version

  • You can use RoboNexus without an account. As a guest, your notes and favorites stay on your device.
  • If you sign in (with Apple or Google), your notes, favorites and settings sync between your own devices. Nobody else can see them.
  • If you turn on notifications or Live Activities, we store your device's push token and the teams and events you follow so we can send them.
  • We collect anonymous usage analytics and crash reports to improve the app. You can turn analytics off.
  • You can delete your account, and everything synced to it, from inside the app.

What we collect

Location. If you allow it, the app uses your location while you're using it to show nearby events and to pick your country in event search. On iPhone, iPad and Android, your location stays on your device. On the web version, approximate coordinates are sent to OpenStreetMap's Nominatim service to look up your country.

Camera and photos. The camera and photo picker are used only when you choose to attach a photo to a note. Photos stay on your device.

Competition data. Team numbers, team names, match results, rankings, awards and game manuals shown in the app come from public competition sources (the REC Foundation and VEX Robotics). They are not personal data you provide to us.

How it's stored and shared

Account, sync, notification and Live Activity data is stored on RoboNexus's own servers. Our servers don't keep logs of individual requests; IP addresses are used briefly, in memory, to limit abuse. Requests pass through Cloudflare, which protects and delivers the service.

We share data only with the services that make the app work:

  • Apple and Google, to sign you in and to deliver push notifications and Live Activity updates.
  • Expo, which relays push notifications to Apple and Google.
  • Google Firebase Analytics, for anonymous usage analytics (Google's privacy policy).
  • Cloudflare, which hosts and protects our web services.

We don't sell or rent your data, and we don't share it with advertisers or data brokers.

How long we keep it

  • Account and synced data: until you delete your account.
  • Notification registration: until you turn notifications off or uninstall the app (we remove tokens that Apple or Google report as no longer valid).
  • Live Activity records: deleted 30 days after the Live Activity ends.
  • Analytics and crash reports: kept only as long as they're useful for improving the app, then deleted.

Deleting your data

  • Delete your account: in the app, go to Settings → Data → Account & Sync → Delete account. This permanently deletes your account and everything synced to it from our servers. If you signed in with Apple, it also revokes RoboNexus's access to your Apple ID. Notes already on your devices stay there until you remove them or uninstall the app.
  • Stop notifications: turn them off in the app's settings; your device's registration is removed from our servers.
  • Stop analytics: turn off "Share usage analytics" in Settings → Data → Privacy.
  • You can also email skylerclagg1@gmail.com to ask for a copy of your data or for it to be deleted.

Children

Many RoboNexus users are students. The app works without an account, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Signing in is optional; if you are under 13, ask a parent or guardian first. If you believe a child has given us personal information, email us and we'll delete it.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS), and sign-in sessions are stored in your device's secure storage. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to protect your information.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page. Significant changes may also be announced in the app.
